# Methodology

## Cadence
- Up to 6 third-party PRs/day across 6 repos
- ≥4 hours between upstream opens
- Warm forks hours before upstream; never fork→PR in <90s
- Stay under public automation rate thresholds

## Open
Famous mains (≥1000★) with regression-tested product bugs: quoting, argv, paths, parsers, build/packaging wrappers.

## Refuse
Hard AI bans, AgentScan circles, satellites-as-redirect, same-day pairs, docs farms, GFI farming where banned, re-entering closed-unmerged repos.

## Review
CI green; reply to technical review with SHAs; never @-nudge. Hostility/AI attacks: do not argue — leave/close and record forever-out.
